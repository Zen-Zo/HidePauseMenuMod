# Hide Pause Menu

Adds a hide button to the pause menu, for getting a better look at your deaths or capturing clean screenshots.

Check the mod settings, the mod is not configured by default.

## Settings

- **Configurable button position:** place the hide button on the left, bottom, or right side of the pause menu, or turn it off.
- **End Level Menu:** the same hide behavior can be applied to the end level screen.
- **Keybind support:** hide or toggle hide the menu visibility using custom keybinds.
- **Auto-hide HUD elements:** optionally hide the FPS counter, progress bar, percentage, timer, attempt counter, practice buttons, audio visualizer, and more when the menu is hidden.

## Usage

1. Pause in a level and press the hide button to toggle menu visibility.
2. Open Geode settings for this mod to configure:
    - Button position (Left / Bottom / Right / Off)
    - End level menu behavior
    - Hide and toggle hide keybinds
    - Auto-hide HUD elements

To hide your new bests you can use:

<mod:hbg1010.hidenewbest>
