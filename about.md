# <c-ffcc4d>Hide Pause Menu</c>

<cy>Adds a hide button to the pause menu</c>, for getting a better look at your deaths or capturing clean screenshots. <cg>(Also works on the hide End Level Menu button)</c>

<cr>Check the mod settings, the mod is not configured by default.</c>

## <c-58a6ff>Settings</c>

- <c-7ee787>Configurable button position:</c> place the hide button on the left, bottom, or right side of the pause menu, or turn it off.
- <c-7ee787>End Level Menu:</c> the same hide behavior can be applied to the end level screen.
- <c-7ee787>Keybind support:</c> hide or toggle hide the menu visibility using custom keybinds.
- <c-7ee787>Auto-hide HUD elements:</c> optionally hide the FPS counter, progress bar, percentage, timer, attempt counter, practice buttons, audio visualizer, and more when the menu is hidden.

<cy>To hide your new bests you can use:</c>

<mod:hbg1010.hidenewbest>
